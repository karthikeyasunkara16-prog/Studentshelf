About StudentShelf

StudentShelf is a peer-to-peer marketplace built exclusively for verified College Students.

Our mission is to promote campus sustainability and savings by enabling students to trade textbooks, electronics, and essentials safely and efficiently within the college community.

By reusing resources, we not only save money but also reduce our environmental footprint. Join us in building a smarter, greener campus!
